# Redaction Checklist

Complete before indexing or export.

- [ ] Remove secrets, tokens, credentials, and keys.
- [ ] Remove private local paths.
- [ ] Remove raw transcript content.
- [ ] Remove personal identifiers not required for the task.
- [ ] Replace sensitive client details with approved labels.
- [ ] Confirm the remaining content matches its memory route.
- [ ] Confirm the source is present in the opt-in list.
